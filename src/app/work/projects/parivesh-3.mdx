## Overview

Parivesh 3.0 is a workflow portal designed to make environmental clearance processes more structured and transparent. Built during the E-SUMMIT 2026 hackathon, the platform models multiple stakeholder roles and brings document integrity and geographic context into one system.

## Key Features

- Role-based workflows for applicants, reviewers, and administrative stakeholders.
- Document hashing to help verify that submitted records have not been altered.
- GIS mapping to connect clearance workflows with relevant environmental and geographic data.
- Responsive interfaces and backend APIs designed around clear workflow states.

## Technologies

React, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, REST APIs, document hashing, and GIS mapping.
